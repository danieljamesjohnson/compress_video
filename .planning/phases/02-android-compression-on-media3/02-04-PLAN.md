<objective>
Make the two behaviours that define this package's promise real and measured: the output is never
larger than the input, and a clip that already meets the target is remuxed instead of re-encoded.
Then replace the provisional preset numbers with measured ones and write them down.

Purpose: "52 MB video, 15 minutes, 82 MB output" is a real issue on the incumbent's tracker, and the
reason it happened is that its presets are resolution rules with an estimated bitrate and nothing
ever compares the result against the input. The remux fast path is the other half: the incumbent's
Android engine sometimes passes the video through untouched and still reports it as compressed, with
no way for a caller to tell. Both are fixed here by deciding in one place, reporting honestly, and
asserting the speed claim as a ratio measured on the same device in the same run.
Output: transmux and never-larger implemented and proven on the corpus, and a `doc/PRESETS.md`
generated from measurement that Phase 6's README is built from.

## Flagged assumptions — unresolved probe edges (CORE-05, CORE-06)

The deterministic edge probe classified both of these requirements' edges as `unclassified`. They are
deliberately NOT auto-resolved. The assumptions this plan proceeds on are recorded so the verifier can
challenge them rather than inherit them:

- **CORE-05.** The assumption is that "never larger" is decided on the total file byte count, and that
  equality counts as larger — a result equal to the input in bytes is substituted with the original.
  The alternative reading is that only a strictly larger result should be substituted, which would let
  a pointless re-encode ship at the same size with worse quality. Surface this to the developer; do
  not close it by fiat.
- **CORE-06.** The assumption is that "a fraction of the encode time" means strictly less than 30
  percent of the elapsed time of a comparable encode on the same device, as 02-CONTEXT.md's own
  acceptance bar states, and that a transmux of a clip that also qualifies for never-larger takes the
  transmux path. If a device exists where a remux is not dramatically faster, this threshold is wrong
  rather than the implementation. Surface it; do not close it.

## The decision order this plan fixes

Documented here because it is not obvious and because two plausible orders give opposite results for
`small_480p.mp4`:

1. **Transmux first.** If the input's codec, audio codec, displayed long side, frame rate and bitrate
   all already satisfy the resolved target, and no trim and no audio change is requested, the job
   remuxes. `transmuxed: true`, `usedOriginal: false`.
2. **Never-larger pre-check second.** Otherwise, if the predicted encode output is greater than or
   equal to the input's byte count, the encode is skipped entirely and the original is copied to the
   output path. `usedOriginal: true`, `transmuxed: false`.
3. **Encode last**, followed by the never-larger post-check on the real byte count.

At `CompressPreset.p720`, `small_480p.mp4` satisfies every transmux condition and takes path 1. At
`CompressPreset.p360` its 854-pixel long side exceeds the 640-pixel target, so transmux is off the
table, the predicted encode is larger than the 77,504-byte input, and it takes path 2. One clip, two
options, both behaviours — which is why 02-CONTEXT.md names it as the never-larger fixture and why it
also serves as the transmux fixture.
