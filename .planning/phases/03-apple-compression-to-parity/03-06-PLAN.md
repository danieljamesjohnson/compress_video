<objective>
Give every Apple job its own honest progress, a cancel that actually stops work and cleans up, and
a typed failure for every way a compression can go wrong.

Purpose: these are the three behaviours a caller notices when they go wrong, and all three are
where the incumbent this package replaces failed hardest — a global progress stream, a cancel that
left partial files, and errors swallowed into null. Phase 2 established the exact semantics on
Android, including two ordering subtleties that are easy to re-break: that 100 is sent once
explicitly rather than derived, and that the partial file is gone before the future resolves. This
plan reproduces those semantics on Apple and proves them with the same suite.
Output: progress derivation, cancellation, the wired error taxonomy and the free-space pre-check,
with `compress_jobs_test.dart` green on the iOS simulator and the macOS host.
</objective>
