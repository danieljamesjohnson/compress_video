## Flagged assumptions this plan records rather than resolves

Two properties of the CI design are asserted by construction rather than by a test that
deliberately breaks the build, and are recorded as backstop-verified in `must_haves.truths`:

- **In-progress cancellation on a second push to the same ref.** Proving it requires two pushes
  timed within one run's lifetime, which is a flaky thing to gate on and burns runner minutes. The
  `concurrency` declaration is asserted by a source check; the behaviour is recorded as backstop.
- **The workflow concluding `failure` whenever either job fails, regardless of completion order.**
  Proving it requires deliberately breaking a job. The independence of the two jobs is asserted by
  a source check (neither appears in the other's `needs`); the ordering-independent conclusion is
  recorded as backstop.
